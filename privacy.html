Privacy

Last updated 2 July 2026

The short version: no cookies, no tracking, no personal data — just an anonymous count of how many people visit.

EdFringeNow is a static website. It runs entirely in your browser. There are no accounts, no sign-up, and no server of ours behind it — so there is nothing about you for us to receive, log, or keep.

We don't sell your data, and we don't track you across the web. No ads. No advertising trackers. No cookies set by us. The one thing we do measure is anonymous, aggregate visitor numbers — see Analytics below.

Analytics

To understand roughly how many people use EdFringeNow, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics — a privacy-first tool chosen precisely because it doesn't use cookies, doesn't store personal data, and can't identify you or follow you to other sites. It records anonymous, aggregate signals only: page views, the referring site, and a broad region worked out from your request (your IP address is used to derive that, but is never stored). No cross-site tracking, no advertising profile — which is also why there's no cookie-consent banner to click through.

Your location

If you allow location access, your position is used only in your browser to work out which shows are near you. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deny it and the app simply falls back to central Edinburgh — everything still works.

What your browser loads

Like any web page, opening EdFringeNow fetches a few files from other services. Those services see your IP address and browser as part of a normal web request, the same as visiting any website:

  • Map tiles from OpenStreetMap.
  • The map library (unpkg) and typefaces (Google Fonts) from their content delivery networks.
  • The privacy-first analytics beacon (a small script) from Cloudflare.

The show and venue listings are plain files served from this site. They describe the festival, not you.

Links out

“Open in Maps” opens Google Maps, and ticket links open edfringe.com. Once you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies — not this one.
