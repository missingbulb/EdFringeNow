# Finder run, 2026-09-29

The first run, which also set the lists up. Scope: academic conferences worldwide with Israel
first, the discovery method for every kind, and Israeli festivals of every kind from
October 2026 to September 2027. Three research passes worked in parallel.

## What each route yielded

- **eventact** (Israeli conference organisers): 15 upcoming Israeli events across Ortra,
  Comtec and Paragon, one with a published per-session timetable (the Association for Israel
  Studies conference, 106 sessions in 11 halls). Its agenda API is the one ISRA already uses.
- **pretalx**: 87 hosted events from October 2026 on, all fetched in one call. Of the few
  schedules fetched, Hack.lu, PyConES and MarxismNL are published; the academic societies'
  (World History Association, DVRW, Africa Bioethics, ANZSOC) answer 404 until release.
- **tel-aviv-cinematheque**: TLVFest's programme is live there (not on tlvfest.com), plus
  the Soundtrack, Animix and children's festivals on the same template.
- **ticketing-fingerprint**: a platform link on 8 of 11 Israeli festival sites; Red Sea Jazz
  and the Eilat Piano Festival sell on Ticketmaster Israel.
- **Hebrew and English searches**: Israman's full race weekend, the Tel Aviv and Jerusalem
  marathons, the Israel Physical Society meeting.
- **Nothing**: Indico (no Israeli events upcoming), conferenceindex (paper-mill listings),
  science.co.il (stale), HUJI IIAS (one-day workshops), the IMA events page (archive only),
  the eventer.co.il search URL (404), twelve guessed Israeli festival domains (none exist).
