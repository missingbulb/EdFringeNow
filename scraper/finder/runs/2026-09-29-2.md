# Finder run, 2026-09-29 (second)

Scope: the first run's "next run" list. That meant three passes in parallel:
- the platforms that imply a per-session schedule (pretalx, Eventive, Sched), worldwide;
- Israeli academic and medical societies and conference organisers;
- the Israeli festival leads the first run left unworked.

Window: October 2026 to September 2027. The run grew the lists from 67 to 172 candidates and
from 41 to 53 sources. Of the 105 new candidates, 41 have a published programme.

## What each route yielded

- **pretalx**: 739 hosted events in one unpaged call, 87 in the window.
  - 14 published schedules, 9 still 404 (watch).
  - The four academic societies the first run watched are still 404.
- **pretalx-instances** (new): a certificate-transparency search (`crt.sh`) found 235
  `pretalx.*` hosts, and 57 of them answer the events API.
  - 4 published, 6 watch, among them the Chaos Communication Congress on cfp.cccv.de.
- **eventive**: 7 film festivals published, found by `site:` search.
- **eventive-discovery** (new): the discovery page lists 197 tenants.
  - 4 published, 2 watch; Sundance 2027 is watched until its schedule replaces the placeholders.
- **sched**: 6 published through `all.ics`; 2 watch, where the file has no sessions yet.
- **ima-events-calendar** (new): the Israel Medical Association's calendar, about 75
  upcoming meetings on one server-rendered page.
  - It surfaced 15 of the 24 society candidates, 3 with a programme out (neurology,
    anaesthesiology, emergency medicine).
- **forms-wizard**, **think-eventact**, **bgu-conferences**, **kenes-israel**, **kld-events**
  (new): conference organisers and a registration platform; the BGU portal listed nothing
  upcoming.
- **weizmann-conferences**: reachable again, but its meetings are nearly all single-hall
  workshops.
- **il-listing-sites** (Mevalim) and the first run's leads: 11 leads became 11 entries.
  - 5 rejected: too few sessions or one stage, or no 2026 edition.
  - The rest are watched.
- **hebrew-wikipedia-festival-categories** (new): 11 categories, 122 titles.
  - The category is `ישראל: פסטיבלים`, not `פסטיבלים בישראל`.
  - Six titles worked into candidates; the rest are listed in the source's `how`.
- **smarticket**: regional council storefronts carry whole festival programmes.
  - Kol HaMusica in the Upper Galilee is published there.
- **beit-tziyonei-america** and **jerusalem-cinematheque** (new): venue hubs.
  - Beit Tziyonei America gave the HaSha'a children's theatre festival, published.
  - The Jerusalem Cinematheque hosts the Jerusalem Film Festival, watched for July 2027.
- **sgcaptcha-hosted-sites** (new, blocked): Jaffa Theatre, Hebrew Book Week and the
  group-therapy association answer HTTP 202 captchas on every path.

## Traps learned

- `jff.org.il` refuses curl's default user agent only; a browser user agent gets a 200.
- `jbookforum.com` now serves gambling spam, and Suzanne Dellal's site carries casino links in
  its menu. Neither is the festival's own page any more.
- Many 2026 Israeli editions moved because of the war, so a 2027 date cannot be inferred from
  2026.
- Timestamps from Eventive and Sched are UTC. The traps for each platform are now in its
  source's `how`.

## Found nothing

- Cancer research, philosophy, psychology and archaeology societies.
- Diesenhaus, Target, and the Kenes Group calendar (no Israeli meetings in the window).
- The Technion, Bar-Ilan, Tel Aviv and Haifa university event pages: they are rendered by
  script, return 404 or reset the connection.
- Official sites for Genesis and the Tel Aviv Jazz Festival.
- The Haifa municipality calendar answers 482, and tel-aviv.gov.il item pages are an empty
  JavaScript shell.
- A sweep of every EventAct company id through the event-list API was refused by the
  session's permission check and not attempted another way.
