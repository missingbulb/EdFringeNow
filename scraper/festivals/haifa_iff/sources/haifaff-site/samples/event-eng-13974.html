    Short from the Past 1 – A Selection of Israeli Short Films from Recent Years

    Outdoor Events

    Event Details

    Cinema Reshet - Herzl 46

    Shorts from the Past 1 – A Selection of Israeli Short Films

    The first collaboration between Cinema Reshet and the Haifa International Film Festival brings a special selection of Israeli short films that have screened at the festival in recent years.
    Join us for three evenings of short films in a unique outdoor screening in the heart of Haifa’s Hadar neighborhood.

    Cinema Reshet – 46 Herzl St., Haifa
    Doors open: 7:30 PM
    Screening starts: 8:30 PM

    Tickets available on-site only
    20 NIS | Seating is first come, first served

    From Here, You Can See the View
    First screened at the 36th Haifa International Film Festival – 2020
    16 min.

    Directed, written, shot and edited by Tal Elkim
    Co-edited by Naama Shmueli
    Sound design: Neil Gibbs

    A student film produced at the Sam Spiegel Film School.

    Between everyday life and national memorial days, between flowers and memorials to victims of terror, the Armon HaNetziv Promenade becomes a canvas for an Israeli identity caught between celebration, commemoration and confusion.

     

    In the Shadow of the Mountain / Maya Kassel
    First screened at the 39th Haifa International Film Festival – 2023
    22 min.

    Directed and written by Maya Kassel
    Produced by Tal Moskowitz and Matan Glazovsky – Daisy Films
    Cinematography: Gal Rombek
    Editing: Gal El-Ad
    Sound design: Michael Emet
    Music: Adam Weingrod
    Featuring: Mingze Zhang, Maya Yang-Lazar, Li Le, Hertzel Tobi

    In the middle of a workday, Ming is pulled away from a light-rail construction site and taken to a place that brings back memories of the home he left behind.

    Event Horizon / Shira Geffen
    First screened at the 40th Haifa International Film Festival – 2024/25
    15 min.

    Directed and written by Shira Geffen
    Produced by Stav Morg Maron – Pardes Films
    Cinematography: Daniela Novitz
    Editing: Mai Resh
    Sound design: Aviv Aldema
    Music: Tamuz Dekel
    Featuring: Eldad Fribas, Tinker Shirai, Amelie Weiss, Timor Cohen

    Five-year-old Noga joins her family on a trip through the desert. As her parents’ tension and constant arguments clash with the guide’s attempts to maintain an atmosphere of normality and stability, Noga finds herself caught between the two. Through her eyes, an ordinary family trip becomes an unexpected journey into adulthood.

    More screenings in the Shorts from the Past series:

    Wednesday, September 30 – Program 2
    Cold Water – Itamar Elkalkai | Salma – Suheila Gattas | A Fading Memory – Yiftach Samocha

    Thursday, October 1 – Program 3
    White Eye – Tomer Sasson | It’s Time You Died Already – Hadar Morag | The Day My Father Dies – Naif Hamoud


    The event is presented in collaboration with Cinema Reshet, an independent cinema in Haifa’s Hadar neighborhood that brings people together through local filmmaking and creates a space for conversation, creativity and culture rooted in the here and now.

    Follow Cinema Reshet on Instagram and WhatsApp.
