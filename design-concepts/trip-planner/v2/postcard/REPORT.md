# Postcard — designer's report
Idea: the trip is a physical postcard on a kraft scrapbook page. Each wizard question is an index card laid over it; answering turns the question into a sticker on the card (pin, stamps, postmark, family, wallet, sprinter, train, guesthouse). The draft is the postcard turned over: five ruled days of a handwritten itinerary with illustrated ticket stubs; peeling a stub reveals the correction stickers.

Screens: 01-opening (Where to? + When? — festival stamps, postmark dates, "Also on…" stamp sheet), 02-question (Who's coming? — illustrated radio, stacked cards still to answer, priorities already stuck on), 03-schedule (answers collapsed into a sticker strip; postcard back with the draft; "Stick on…" sheet for day out / meal / pick myself), 04-correct (Comedy Club 4 Kids peeled: not-this-time / not-this-show / no-comedy / not-Pleasance / keep-it; the Tattoo peels to "Not for me" only).

UI copy words (excluding ticket/address/calendar content): 01: 33 · 02: 35 · 03: 60 · 04: 73. Ticket content on 03/04: 158 words across 21 stubs.

Decisions to judge:
1. Dates are the postmark, festivals are the stamps — no date pickers or checkboxes; the cancellation mark over the stuck-on stamps carries the dates and the festivals chosen.
2. Corrections are stickers under a peeled ticket, not a menu; starred tickets wear a gold star and never peel; one-off events reveal a single "Not for me".
3. Fonts: Caveat handwriting + Nunito; layouts were tuned against the wider fallback.
