# Journey map — designer's report
The wizard is a dotted route from home to Edinburgh and back; each question is a stop on it, answered stops shrink to an illustrated marker of what was chosen, and the finished route reveals the draft as a strip of travel-guide pages.

Screens: 01-opening ("Where to?": four picture ways in, Edinburgh with festival stickers, dates + August strip), 02-question ("Who's coming?": four peg-people options, "With kids" chosen with age chips), 03-schedule (route complete; five day-pages with big serif day numbers, a tinted illustration per day, ticket stubs; Day out / A table / Pick shows myself row), 04-correct (a stub folded open into Not now / Not this show / No kids' shows / Not Pleasance / Keep it; the one-off Tattoo opens to just Not interested / Keep it).

Word counts (UI copy / sample content): 01 — 35/0; 02 — 22/0; 03 — 35/123; 04 — 51/123.

Decisions to judge:
1. Answered stops become markers of the answer (family, wallet·sprinter·skyline, train, guesthouse), so the map is the whole trip summary — no stepper.
2. The draft is a guide strip, not a time grid: stubs carry time and price in the perforated tab, star = never swapped, "1×" = one-off; the correction folds out sideways from the stub.
3. Palette slate/heather/gold/cream with coral as the single "you are here" accent; DM Serif Display + DM Sans.
