# Review — classic UX researcher

## Postcard
1. Vacation feel: yes. The postmark carrying the dates over stuck-on festival stamps is the best holiday detail in the set. What breaks it: the postcard's back (03/04) — 21 stubs with sub-lines turn the card into a timetable.
2. Tokens: the 03 sticker strip is legible (picture + word, peeled corner). Screen 02 is not: the answered priorities sit half-hidden under the question card, indistinguishable from decoration. Nothing says any token reopens.
3. Pictures: Season = sun reads as "weather"; "City too" is mush at 40px; the comedy masks serve as the Fringe stamp, the Genre option and "No comedy" — one glyph, three meanings.
4. Text: too much. 04 is 73 UI words plus 158 in stubs. Worst offender: stub sub-lines.
5. Correction: the curl exists only on the already-lifted ticket, so at rest nothing invites the peel; the sticker row then covers Saturday. The Tattoo's "Not for me" floats under Monday's train stub.
6. Fixes: dog-ear every ticket at rest; delete stub sub-lines; anchor "Not for me" to the Tattoo stub and give "No comedy" its own glyph.

## Journey map
1. Vacation feel: partly. The route and the tinted day-page headers are the most holiday-like pieces anywhere here. The panel beneath — ARRIVE/LEAVE fields, 31-box strip, "NOTHING BOOKED YET" pill — is the enterprise form the owner rejected.
2. Tokens: best of the three. Answered stops show a tick and the answer; unanswered ones sit dashed and grey ahead on the same line, so past/now/next is one glance. Weak: 10px grey tracked caps are barely legible, and nothing marks a marker as tappable.
3. Pictures: peg-people and the Tattoo drum read instantly. The merged priorities marker (wallet·sprinter·skyline at 34px) carries nothing. "Free 15:00" with a tent icon is ambiguous: free of charge, or a free slot?
4. Text: right. Worst offender: ellipsised second lines — cut text is text to remove.
5. Correction: the fold-out and the Tattoo "Not interested / Keep it" are clear once open; a stub at rest has no cue. The red "1×" badge reads "1 problem", not "one night only".
6. Fixes: replace ARRIVE/LEAVE + strip with a picture calendar; recolour "1×" to a grey "once" tag; labels to 13px ink, and drop truncated lines.

## One big question
1. Vacation feel: yes, strongest. Suitcase → four moons → waving hand says "4 nights" without writing it. Risk: the midnight-indigo 03/04 sits closer to a dashboard than the yellow/coral before it.
2. Tokens: read as progress, not as reopenable — filled circles look like earned badges, and the empty dashed circles are a bare stepper with no hint what comes. No tap signal anywhere.
3. Pictures: best-drawn set; party cards and the correction 2×2 need no reading. Fails: Tattoo = castle + fireworks when castle already means Edinburgh; the Tattoo tile's asterisk reads as a snowflake.
4. Text: right (16/16/34/48).
5. Correction: once flipped, clearest of the three, and "One night only" over the single option is the best one-off treatment. But long-press is undiscoverable and does not exist on desktop, and the no-badge on "Pleasance" is small enough that a hurried user may think they are confirming the venue.
6. Fixes: a visible tab (dog-ear or ⋯) on every tile instead of long-press; empty circles become faint pictures of the questions to come; swap the Tattoo castle for a drum.

## Ranking
1. One big question — meets the owner's headline standard; its faults are affordance fixes, not structure.
2. Journey map — the most usable wizard and cleanest copy, but one panel is the rejected corporate form.
3. Postcard — the richest metaphor and the worst usability: hidden peel, over-budget text, orphaned popover, tokens lost under the card.

## Steal into One big question
- Journey's route as the token row: unanswered stops carry a faint picture of the question ahead — a journey, not a stepper.
- Journey's tinted per-day illustration header on each day card.
- Postcard's curled sticker corner on every schedule tile as the "lift me" affordance.
