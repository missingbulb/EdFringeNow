Four ways the next show could find you

Same shows, same moment — four very different answers to the one question the app exists for: what can I catch right now, near here, and still make my next thing? Each is a clickable, self-contained mockup. Open them on a phone-width window for the intended feel.

🕒 Now 15:10 📍 Bristo Square 🚶 Walking · ≤15 min 🎭 Comedy & Cabaret 🍽 Dinner 18:30 · Dishoom
Concept A

Departure Board

Your shows as a live transit departures board — ranked by urgency, each a boarding pass with a countdown and an honest “you’ll still make dinner” cushion.

Leads with
An urgency-sorted feed (no map)
Best for
The in-a-hurry glance — what do I bolt to now?
Feel
Dark transit board, split-flap, mono numerals
View concept Concept B

Map-first bottom sheet

The map is the answer: a calm Citymapper-style map with colour-coded reachability pins and a draggable sheet that traces your walk there and onward to dinner.

Leads with
A full-bleed map + draggable sheet
Best for
Orientation & the two-leg journey
Feel
Pale-mint cartography, soft floating UI
View concept Concept C

Deal Me a Show

A joyful Fringe-poster take built around “deal me a show” — it lands on one reachable pick as a torn ticket stub: you can make this, leave by ~15:25, back for dinner.

Leads with
A serendipity result (one dealt show)
Best for
“Just pick one I can make” — no choosing
Feel
Warm riso print, oversized poster type
View concept Concept D

Calm Timeline

A calm, Swiss timeline that drops one show into your 3pm-to-dinner gap and proves it fits — with a green slack-cushion bar and a plain two-leg plan to Dishoom.

Leads with
A journey rail: now → your pick → dinner
Best for
Trust — fitting a show into a real gap
Feel
Airy paper, one accent, editorial serif
