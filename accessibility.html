Accessibility

Last updated 2 July 2026

We want EdFringeNow to be usable by everyone — on a phone, on a street, in a hurry.

What we've done

  • Full keyboard navigation, with a clearly visible focus outline on every control.
  • Semantic markup and ARIA labels, so a screen reader can announce the plan, the filters, and the list of shows.
  • Respects your device's “reduce motion” setting — animations switch off when you ask them to.
  • Large tap targets and a single, legible column that scales down to small screens.

What we're aiming for

We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 AA. We're a small project and we won't always hit it perfectly.

Found a barrier?

If anything is hard to use with your assistive technology, please tell us at support@edfringenow.com — say what you were trying to do and what got in the way, and we'll fix it.
